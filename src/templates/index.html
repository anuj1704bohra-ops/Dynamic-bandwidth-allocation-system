Queue Dynamics

Each traffic class maintains a finite-capacity drop-tail buffer. Incoming traffic bits exceeding buffer capacity are dropped and recorded as packet drops. Allocated capacity drains the buffer up to the slot's byte budget.

Estimated Delay Metric

Queueing delay (ms) is computed from the backlog divided by scheduled service rate: Delay = (Backlog / Allocation) × 1000 ms. When backlog is zero, delay is 0.0 ms.

Policy Equality & Fair Comparison

All dynamic policies (Neural, Moving Average, Last-Observed) employ the exact same weighted water-filling allocation algorithm with identical priority weights, isolating forecast accuracy as the sole independent variable.
